Kōshi Sugawara (Japanese: 菅原 孝支 Sugawara Kōshi) was a third-year student at Karasuno High. He was the vice captain, a substitute setter, and pinch server for the Boys' Volleyball Team.

As of 2018, he is an elementary school teacher in Miyagi.
