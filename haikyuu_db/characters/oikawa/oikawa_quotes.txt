(To Kageyama) "Yoohoo! Long time no see, Tobio-chan. So nice to see you. You still doing the King thing?"[11]

(To Aoba Johsai before their match with Karasuno) "I believe in all of you."[12]

(To himself) "Even if I'm no match for his [Kageyama's] talent, I have confidence that I can set the best tosses for everyone. That's why I won't lose as a setter."[13]

(To himself) "Spikers are all different. Once you're able to bring out 100% of the strengths of each and every one of them, that's when you'll be a true setter!"[13]

(To Iwaizumi) "Once we get into high school...that's when we'll [himself and Iwaizumi] show Shiratorizawa who's boss!"[14]

(To Kageyama) "...And Tobio-chan, I don't know where you'll be headed after this [his last junior high tournament], but I'm gonna crush you, so you better be prepared!"[14]

(To Iwaizumi) "My perfect trust with you, Iwa-chan."[14]

(To Iwaizumi) "All of a sudden, I feel invincible."[14]

(To Kageyama) "Tobio, I might actually lose to you, considering how quickly you evolve, but... that won't be today!"[15]

(To Ushijima) "I'm going to crush you today. You'd better be prepared, Ushiwaka-chan."[16]

(To himself during the Interhigh tournament match against Shiratorizawa) "All of our [Aoba Johsai] practices, experience, and strategies are being broken by force. Well, I guess the actual path to victory doesn't matter. The one that lets the ball fall on the court loses. That's everything."[16]

(To Kageyama) "The one who has leadership in an attack isn't you, it's the shrimp."[17]

(To himself) "Just because a super receive is flashy, doesn't mean it's good. If a ball that had always gone up for them suddenly doesn't go up anymore, that will slowly but surely get to them. I think they're feeling that right about now."[18]

(To Kindaichi about Kageyama) "Tobio's genius-like qualities don't lie in his techniques, but rather how stupid he is. Where others would probably hold back, he goes straight for it. In both a good and bad way."[19]

(To Aoba Johsai) "The chance to let your talent truly blossom, perhaps it's today. Or maybe, it's tomorrow, the day after tomorrow, or next year. Or perhaps even when you're 30? In regards to physique, I can't say much. But if you yourself think that you don't have talent, then you'll probably never have it."[19]

(To Karasuno) "Six who are strong together, are stronger!"[19]

(To Ushijima) "Worthless pride, huh? That's true... Listen up, Ushijima. I never thought my decision was wrong, and my volleyball hasn't ended at all. Don't you ever forget my worthless pride."[20]

(To Iwaizumi) "No matter which side wins, I'm gonna be able to see the other team's faces when they lose."[21]

(To Aoba Johsai's third-years) "Thank you for these three years!!!"[22][23]

(Hinata to himself about Oikawa) "He thought through to 'what comes next' at every step. No matter where in the world he is, no matter how many people are on his team, he doesn't change."[2]

(To himself) "When you dedicate yourself to pushing higher, you're going to have to expect the hard times to outweigh the fun. It gets to the point where you begin to believe that if it isn't hard, if it isn't rough, then you aren't giving everything you can. Yet, without any rhyme or reason, from time to time, something fun will just hit… out of nowhere. It's that fun that keeps pulling me onward. Volleyball is fun. Every time I forget that, it comes back to remind me."[9]

(To Hinata) "M'kay, just remember… I'm going to beat everyone. Be ready."[24]
