Favorite Food: Strawberry Shortcake (Japanese: ショートケーキ Shōtokēki).

Pre-timeskip Concern: Upon entering high school, he once again has to put up with various people asking how to read the character for his first name.

Post-timeskip Concern: He wants to improve his serve accuracy a little more.

Star Sign: Libra.

The character for his name (蛍) means firefly and can be read as either Hotaru or Kei.

Tsukishima wears white gym shoes with green accents.

He has the third-smallest appetite in the series after Satori Tendō and Kenma Kozume,[36] often making his teammates and upperclassmen worry for his health.
    Like Kenma and Tendō, he states that he has a normal appetite and others simply eat more than normal.

His BMI is the lowest of the male characters in the series.

He has a habit of fiddling with his fingers when he feels nervous, which is demonstrated most notably when he asks Coach Ukai for advice on blocking.

He is usually seen with his white Sony (Somy in anime to avoid trademark concerns) headphones around his neck whenever he's not practicing or playing.

Tsukishima appears to be good at studying, as he belongs to Class 1-4, known to be a college preparatory class.
    It is confirmed that he shares the same class as Tadashi Yamaguchi.

His birthday falls on the day of the Harvest Moon, which is when the Mid-Autumn Festival, also known as the Moon Festival, on the Chinese calendar is celebrated in September 2015.[37]
    Coincidentally, the first kanji in his last name means "moon".
    In addition, Tsukishima was often seen with a crescent moon symbol on his hoodies and jackets as a child.

He keeps toy dinosaurs on a shelf above his desk. Whether he actually likes dinosaurs or not is unknown, but it can be assumed that he does, as is demonstrated in a Let's! Haikyuu!? chapter.[38]

Tsukishima is often referred to as the moon while Hinata is the sun due to their surnames and opposing personalities.
    It also reflects their playing styles during games as Tsukishima is a quiet strategist and Hinata is the attention drawing decoy.

In a poll conducted by Charapedia, a Japanese anime and manga character database, that asked which anime characters would they choose as the basis for their children's name, Tsukishima came in 9th with 98 votes among female respondents.[39]

In One-Shot (Weekly Shōnen Jump), Tsukishima was a second-year while Hinata and Kageyama were first-years. He was also only 184 cm.

Furudate picked Tsukishima's name because: "His last name has 'moon' (月) to contrast Hinata's 'sun' (日). But I named him 'firefly' (蛍) because he can emit light on his own."[40]

In Haikyuu's first popularity poll, Tsukishima ranked 8th with 3,407 votes.[41] In the second popularity poll, he rose to 4th with 8,305 votes.[42] In the Takara Tomy Character Poll, he ranked 11th with 4,595 votes.

Tsukishima's most hated type of spikers are the ones who ricochet the ball off the blocker's hands. This was noted by him after Ennoshita noticed his expression during the match against Wakutani Minami High[43].

He is the third oldest first-year on the team after Hitoka Yachi and Hinata.

He, Kanji Koganegawa, and Kentarō Kyōtani are currently Sendai Frogs players.
    In the Monster Generation Interview, it's said that the Sendai Frogs are rising in the ranks and may be promoted to Division 1 if the trend continues.

When Hinata returned from Brazil, he gifted Tsukishima a pink and glittery shirt. It is unknown if Tsukishima wore/kept the shirt.

Nomenclature:
    Kei (蛍) – Firefly
    Tsukishima (月島) – Moon Island
        The tsuki in his surname, 月島, is the same as Chiharu Tsukioka's first kanji of his surname. They both mean "moon."
