(To Kageyama and Hinata) "We're not hung up on winning or losing, so if it'll be troublesome for you guys to lose... how about we throw the game for you?"[30]

(To Yamaguchi) "...Uselessly hot-blooded people irritate me..."[30]

(To Kageyama) "You aren't the only one who's thinking out there. What the opponent's defense looks like and how it's reacting. Which shots are working and which aren't. All of us think about that."[26]

(To Yamaguchi about Hinata) "It's not like I'm competing with him in the first place."[27]

(To himself) "God, what's gotten into me? This is stupid. I'm not Hinata. Why do I feel like I want to keep fighting to the end?"[31]

(To Lev) "Roofing someone certainly feels good, yes, but personally, I despise the idea of blockers getting around me even more. Settling down and following the ball means slower reaction times, yes, but that way setters will never shake you completely."[32]

(To Yamaguchi) "It really is irritating to have Hinata buzzing around in front of you all the time like a bug."[33]

(To Tendō) "Blocking is systematic."[34]

(To Karasuno) "The idea that the only good blocking is kill blocking is way outdated."[11]

(To himself thinking about Hinata) "If I could stuff him right here, I bet the whole team would go crazy. But... I can't. So, you had better be there."[35]

(His last lines of the series, to Kyōtani) "I'll watch, I'll watch. There's just something about seeing them out on that court... that makes me feel like I have to get moving too."[29]
