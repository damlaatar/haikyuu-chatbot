(To Hinata) "In the first round, and the second round, and the finals, and all the way up to the Nationals! I'm going to be the one who wins and gets to stay on the court!"[61][62]

(To himself) "No matter how challenging the volley, there's only one reason why we chase the ball...there's only one reason we continue fighting. Because we haven't lost yet!"[61][62]

(To Hinata) "Only the winners get to stay on the court. Only the best. If you want to win, then get stronger! Get better!"[9][63]

(To Hinata) "As long as you're with me, you're the greatest!"[9][63]

(His and Sugawara's dual monologue) "I want to be in the game. [I want to stay here longer. I want to stay with these guys.] Give me the thrill of the court. [I want to fight with these guys longer.] Let me feel out of breath. [Let me stand here.] I want to touch the ball. I want to fight. [I want to throw up a toss with my own hands over and over.] Then, we'll [Karasuno] win the match that's right in front of us!"[64][65]

(To Hinata) "That does it. I'm not apologizing anymore. I'm never gonna put the ball up in such a sorry way... that I have to apologize for it ever again!"[66][67]

(To Ushijima) "If Oikawa-san is the best setter in the prefecture, I'm the one who is going to surpass him."[68][69]

(To himself) "It's one thing when my spike gets blocked, but it pisses me of a million times more when a spike from my set-up gets blocked..."[70][71]

(To Tendō) "'The end of the world?' Why? The only thing that'll ever be like that to me... is the news that I'll never be able to play volleyball ever again. That's it."[72][73]

(To Hinata) "Hey. You can jump even higher."[36][74]

(First in his mind only then the last two words directly to Hinata) "To think the day would come... when I'd say this to 'you'. Nice receive."[75][76]

(To himself) "I know, now. All of you taught it to me... so I know it must be true! Now, just like then, my hitters are 'there'. And they're waiting... for the best set that I can give them!"[77]

(To himself) "Hitters are so cool. The cooler the hitters are... the happier I am as a setter."[54]
