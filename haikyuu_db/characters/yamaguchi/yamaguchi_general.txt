Tadashi Yamaguchi (Japanese: 山口 忠 Yamaguchi Tadashi) was a first-year student at Karasuno High. He was a middle blocker and pinch server for the Boys' Volleyball Team. During his third-year, he became the team captain.

He is currently a college student and is planning to work for a home electronics company in Miyagi once he graduates.
