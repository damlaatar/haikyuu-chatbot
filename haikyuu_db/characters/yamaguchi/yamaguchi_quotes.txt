(To himself) "This line [of the volleyball court]... everything beyond this line is a different world."[12][13]

(To himself) "It feels like the temperature on this side [of the volleyball court]... is different."[12][13]

(To himself as he was about to attempt a jump floater serve during the Interhigh Tournament match with Aoba Johsai) "Show them [Aoba Johsai] that you can fight, too!"[12][13]

(To Shimada) "Up until junior high, I thought it was fun just to play with everyone. That was what volleyball was to me. But that's not it... that's not it... I want to be like the others, and... I want to be able to make my body move right! I want to control the ball! I want to play on the same level as the other, better players!"[14][15]

(To Tsukishima about Kageyama) "What can Kageyama do to keep his setting from being read like that? Should he do what Hinata does and do some kind of crazy antsy dance first to throw them off?"[16]

(To himself during his third service ace against Aoba Johsai) "I only have one weapon: my serve. I'm going to fight putting my pride and the flow of the game into that shot. I am ... a pinch server."[17]

(Various, towards Tsukishima) "Sorry, Tsukki!"
