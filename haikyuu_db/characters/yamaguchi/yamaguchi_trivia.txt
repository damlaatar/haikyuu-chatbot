Favorite Food: Fluffy French Fries (Japanese: ふにゃふにゃになったフライドポテト Funya-funya Ninatta Furaidopoteto).

Current Concern: Whenever he thinks a cute girl has come over to talk to him, all they want to talk about is Tsukishima.

Star Sign: Scorpio.

Worst Subject: Since he's in Class 4 with Tsukishima, he is speculated to be smart since Classes 4 and 5 are college-preparatory classes in Karasuno, although he himself has stated that he is terrible at English.
    It is confirmed that he shares the same class as Kei Tsukishima.

He is the second youngest first-year on the team after Tobio Kageyama.

His original reason for joining a sports club was so he could grow stronger. He chose volleyball specifically because the other sports clubs had "scary-looking guys."
    He had first thought of joining baseball or soccer.[1]

Yamaguchi is the only first-year player to not be a regular on the volleyball team.

There has been debate over whether the dotting on his face is acne or freckles, but many believe that the specks are freckles. This is supported, as he was seen with the specks when he first met Tsukishima in grade school.
    In Haikyuu!! Radio #22, Yamaguchi's voice actor, Sōma Saitō, described Yamaguchi with the word "freckles" in a guessing game with Kōki Uchiyama, Ayumu Murase, and Kaito Ishikawa.

In the fourth instance of Haikyuu!! Radio, Yamaguchi's voice actor for the anime adaptation, Sōma Saitō, was asked: "What sort of person is your character, Yamaguchi?", to which he responded: "Loves Tsukki very much."

His seiyuu, Sōma Saitō, is also the seiyuu for Twelve, a character in the anime Zankyou no Terror. Number 12 is Yamaguchi's shirt number.

In seasons one and two of the English dubbing of the anime, he is the only first-year, as well as one of few characters, referred to by his first name instead of his last name. During seasons three and four, he is now referred to as Yamaguchi.

He shares his birthday with Kenji Futakuchi.

When asked about Yamaguchi's name origin, Furudate said, "He's 忠 (loyalty), like a loyal dog!"[18]
    忠 is also how "Hachiko" is written. Hachiko is a famously loyal Japanese dog who also shares the same birthday as Yamaguchi.

In Haikyuu's first popularity poll, Yamaguchi ranked 15th with 1,993 votes.[19] In the second popularity poll, he rose to 7th with 6,561 votes.[20] In the Takara Tomy Character Poll, he ranked 15th with 1,717 votes.

Of the first-years, Yamaguchi is the only one who did not join a professional team after graduating.

While it was only seen with Koganegawa, Yamaguchi possibly does not like it when first-years from other schools address Tsukishima as Tsukki. He does not appear bothered by upperclassmen of other schools using the nickname, possibly out of respect for them being older and some more familiar with Tsukishima than other first-years.

Nomenclature:
    Tadashi (忠) – Loyalty, Devotion
    Yamaguchi (山口) – Mountain Entrance
