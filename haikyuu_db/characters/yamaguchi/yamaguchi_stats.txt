Although having played volleyball for several years prior to attending Karasuno, Yamaguchi's skills are relatively average. His basic volleyball skills are on the weaker side as he often failed to perform receives, blocks, and serves properly. While his taller height added to blocks, he was not strong enough to stop stronger players.

Once the team had formed their regular starting line-up and he was the only first-year left out, Yamaguchi became determined to be a player that could help out his team however he could. After seeing Shimada score numerous aces with the jump float serve, he sought the older man's guidance in learning to perform and perfect the serve himself and it eventually became his main weapon and a powerful tool for the team.

Despite his skills as a server improving over time, his body control as a receiver is still lacking, resorting to body saves and unorthodox shots.

Skills

Jump Float Serve:
Tadashi Yamaguchi Cover
This serve is Yamaguchi's main weapon and has helped out Karasuno in many clutch moments. His jump float serves can vary from skimming the top of the net to catching the opponent off guard having to receive from further out before. Under the tutelage of Makoto Shimada, Yamaguchi increases the speed of his serve so that opponents have a harder time reading the trajectory.[10] He also has worked on improving his aim and accuracy to target a specific player or area.

Serve-and-Block Combo: This strategy involves Yamaguchi breaking the opponents' formation with his serves, thereby forcing players out from participating in offense. This would reduce the amount of information Tsukishima needs to process in order to put up an effective block. The two successfully use the serve-and-block technique in the match against Nekoma.[6]

Blocking: The depth of Yamaguchi's blocking abilities is relatively unexplored as he is not often seen getting to block. Despite this, the times that he does get to block, his height adds to the height of the block and was able to get a deflection on Hirugami's spike in the match against Kamomedai.[11] Unfortunately, because he is not as physically strong as some of his teammates and not as knowledgeable of proper blocking, it is easier for opponents to get by his block.

Receives: Similar to Tsukishima, Yamaguchi has been playing volleyball since elementary school but his receives are not reflective of this. Because of this, when faced with a powerful spiker like Iwaizumi, Yamaguchi will resort to using his body to save the ball. At times, he can receive a powerful spike, but he will usually fall over from not having the strength or balance to stay on his feet.
