Nishinoya's skills in defense are said to be unparalleled. The level of proficiency he demonstrates in his position as a libero has caused Daichi to dub him as a "guardian deity", and his skills were also acknowledged by Nekoma's libero, Yaku. He has supremely fast reflexes, swift feet, and flexible arms. He can receive volleys that most teams would usually deem impossible and keep the ball in play and connected. One of his techniques, "Rolling Thunder", is based on him performing a dive-and-roll receive.

Nishinoya is good at acting on the spur of the moment, adapting to the flow of the game as it is required. This turned out to be a vital asset to Karasuno in their revenge game against the famed "Iron Wall", wherein Nishinoya made the split-second decision of using his foot to prevent the ball from hitting the ground since he knew he could not reach it with his hands in time.[30] That move alone earned him the immediate astonishment and heartfelt praise of the entire stadium, including several volleyball veterans, a number of players from rival teams, and even his normally critical coach.

Skills

Libero Toss:
Nishinoya tosses.

Nishinoya distinguishes himself from many other liberoes with his participation in the offense with a libero set he decided to learn after watching Shinji Watari perform the technique. Despite struggling with it initially, he is now capable of jumping behind the attack line and performing a libero toss either to Asahi or his other teammates in a synchronized attack. This is especially helpful under the circumstances where Kageyama or Sugawara are forced to make the first touch. However, the move is not without drawbacks; given that it is effectively an all-out synchronized attack, there won't be anyone to defend if the attack is blocked.[31]

Overhand Receives: Nishinoya originally has an obvious weakness in overhand receives and is particularly weak at receiving jump floaters. Since Karasuno won the Spring Tournament Qualifiers, he began working on his overhand receives with Kinoshita[32] and has made immense improvements since then. While he still had great difficulty with handling jump float serves from Atsumu during Nationals, Nishinoya received help from Kinoshita to better position himself which led to him finally becoming fully capable of handling overhand receives.

Spiking: Since he is a libero, it is against the rules for Nishinoya to spike and it would be considered a foul if he did this in a match. In the extra Nisekyū!!, Nishinoya enters as a spiker in a volleyball beach tournament, where he demonstrates his ability to spike in an attack he called "Small Big Thunder"[33].

Rolling Thunder: Rolling Thunder is really just a rolling receive (you receive then do a roll), but Nishinoya likes calling it Rolling Thunder. On multiple occasions, he has done the move and shouted "Rolling Thunder!". Usually people get weirded out by it. Tsukishima, Yamaguchi, and Tanaka always laugh when he shouts the name. Hinata is the only one who is interested in the move and wants to know how to do it.

Digs/Receives:
Nishinoya and Daichi are the strongest receivers on the team although Nishinoya is commonly known as the team's guardian due to his incredible defense and at times making seemingly impossible saves. He is seen to make receives of serves and spikes from powerful opponents like Oikawa and Ushijima once he was able to adjust to the different power and spin. Nishinoya is also able to use his feet to save a ball if he is aware that he will not be able to get his hands into place. He did this during Karasuno's first match against Date Tech and later during the match against Nekoma.[27]

Court Awareness: Originally, Nishinoya was usually aware of things around him. During the practice match against Date Tech, Kageyama had pointed out that he had unintentionally blocked the path of the back row attacker when making a flashy receive. Nishinoya quickly accepted his mistake and, since then, has been more aware of where he positions himself during plays and will remove himself if he is in the way of one of his teammates; of which can be seen during the Spring Tournament in the match against Nekoma.
