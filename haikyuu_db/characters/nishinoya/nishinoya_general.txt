Yū Nishinoya (Japanese: 西谷 夕 Nishinoya Yū) was a second-year student at Karasuno High. He was the libero for the boys' volleyball team and was referred to as "Karasuno's Guardian Deity" by his teammates, for his remarkable skills.

After high school, Nishinoya retired from volleyball in favor of traveling the world.
