(To Michimiya) "Even if your opponent is so formidable that you definitely don't seem a match for them, if you don't go into it intending to win, you'll never be able to."[16]

(To Karasuno) "We're going to win the first round [of Spring High] for sure!"[36]

(To Michimiya) "Even if we're not confident that we'll win, even if others tell us we don't stand a chance, we must never tell ourselves that."[1]

(To Kageyama and Hinata) "I can't do any fancy plays, but I can give you guys a solid foundation."[39]

(To himself) "If we don't show them what we're made of now, when the hell are we supposed to show it?"[40]

(To Hinata) "It'll be hard without you. There's no denying that. Hinata, we need you, so just eat some food and rest up!"[29]
