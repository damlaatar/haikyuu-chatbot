Karasuno High

Kōshi Sugawara:
Daichi has a close relationship with Sugawara, having been friends since their first year of being at Karasuno. They know each other very well and rely on each other as teammates and friends as they've been through the ups and downs of their team together. Along with Asahi, they often hang out together outside of the team, and they are on a first-name basis. Sugawara also seems to be the only member of Karasuno who can lecture Daichi and occasionally be immune to his intimidation. As the original team setter, Daichi greatly admired and supported Sugawara's skills and his eventual decision to step back as the official team setter in order to give Karasuno a better chance at victory. Even after high school, the two have stayed close friends.

Asahi Azumane:
Daichi has a close relationship with Karasuno's ace spiker and enjoys teasing him; especially about Asahi not appearing like a tough ace. Since knowing each other since their first year at Karasuno, Daichi knows Asahi very well and his issues with self-doubt at being the ace of the team. However, Daichi has full support in Asahi and what he is able to do and constantly lets the ace know this. As such, their relationship did not change when Asahi temporarily left the team and eventually returned. After graduating high school, they continued to remain close friends.

Kiyoko Shimizu: Daichi is close to Kiyoko as he was the one who recruited her into the volleyball club in their first year. They occasionally talk to each other outside of team activities. Daichi also finds her attractive but doesn't actively seek her affections like Tanaka and Nishinoya. Like others on the team, he heavily respects all of the work she does for them and is occasionally shown to be protective of her.

Ryūnosuke Tanaka and Yū Nishinoya: Daichi believes in the two second-years' incredible skills and even considered Nishinoya as the team's "guardian deity". Despite this, he is often exasperated by their wild behavior and lack of responsibility; often times he is seen reeling them in from getting too carried away.

Tobio Kageyama:
Like many others, Daichi initially found Kageyama's behavior somewhat troubling since the setter wasn't able to connect with his teammates early on and his temper caused many near altercations; many of these situations involving Hinata or Tsukishima. As Kageyama started to change to become more of a team player and overcome his troubled past as the King of the Court, Daichi can occasionally be seen with a proud look on his face as he witnessed Kageyama's progress. Daichi does become annoyed when he has to keep an eye on Kageyama and Hinata when they start to argue with one another but knows and supports how well they work together during a match. Throughout their time playing together and after the timeskip, Daichi continues to be impressed by Kageyama's talent and abilities.

Shōyō Hinata:
Although Daichi can sometimes be caught off guard by Hinata's energetic and impulsive nature, the captain greatly supports the first-year. Even knowing how timid and nervous the first-year can be before a match, Daichi makes sure to give him plenty of encouragement so he plays as best as he can. Daichi admires how Hinata is always looking to improve, although he doesn't always agree with the manner in which Hinata will follow in order to make that happen. After Hinata joined the V League, Daichi continued to support Hinata and was impressed at his new overall strength and abilities.

Kei Tsukishima:
Similar to Kageyama, Daichi found Tsukishima's original behavior difficult to handle since the middle blocker was just as unwilling to open up and connect with others. Even with how difficult Tsukishima could be, Daichi still recognizes his abilities and careful nature as a blocker and trusted that he would figure out how to overcome certain difficulties.

Chikara Ennoshita:
Although Ennoshita doesn't trust his abilities as a leader and the team's future captain, Daichi is very supportive of Ennoshita, especially after Karasuno managed to defeat the powerhouse school Wakutani Minami under Ennoshita's leadership. He helps Ennoshita understand his abilities and feels confident in leaving the team to him.

Yui Michimiya:
Daichi and Michimiya are close friends as they attended the same junior high. They are often seen discussing their teams with each other. Daichi is often shown helping Michimiya gain confidence in both herself and her position as the team captain. It has been hinted several times during the series that Michimiya holds feelings for Daichi, but it is unclear if he acknowledges this.

Ittetsu Takeda:
As the team's advisor, Daichi greatly admires everything that Takeda does for the team, such as scheduling practices matches and reconnecting with other schools and former rivals. Although he is aware that Takeda is not very knowledgeable about volleyball, he was still grateful for everything he did for the team including when he was able to convince Ukai to become the coach. When the team eventually lost in the Spring Tournament, Daichi expressed how he felt that it was thanks to Takeda that the team was even able to make it that far and considered having him as their advisor the greatest blessing the team was able to have.

Tokonami High

Hayato Ikejiri:
Daichi and Ikejiri are shown to have attended the same middle school and played on the volleyball team together. They appear to have been close friends with Daichi attempting to inspire Ikejiri and his teammates during matches. After going to different high schools and not having seen each other until their third year, the two have remained good friends and Daichi continued to be supported by his friend.

Nekoma High

Tetsurō Kuroo:
Daichi and Kuroo share a mutual respect as they are both team captains and many times lookout for their teammates and underclassmen. Most of their interactions take place during the training camps where their teams play against one another. It was during one of these camps that Kuroo approached Daichi to apologize for upsetting Tsukishima. Kuroo occasionally teases Daichi for the Karasuno team doing the most penalties during the camps and Daichi later hints to Oikawa that he and his team became stronger thanks to help from Kuroo and Bokuto. Both also share the goal to have their teams face one another at Nationals. After Nekoma's loss at nationals, Kuroo did not hold a grudge against Daichi but instead hugged his friend and even congratulated Karasuno for putting up a good fight against Kamomedai despite their loss. In several moments post-timeskip, the two are shown to have kept in touch as they eagerly greeted each other after the Adlers vs Jackals game and Kuroo would later contact Daichi to reach others regarding help for the All-Stars match.
