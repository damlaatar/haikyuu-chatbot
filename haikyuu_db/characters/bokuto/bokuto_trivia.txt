    Favorite Food: Grilled Meat (Barbecued meat, usually beef) (Japanese: 焼肉 Yakiniku).

    Pre-timeskip Concern: "... How do you spell 'concern'?"

    Post-timeskip Concern: He's not really sure about taxes and stuff.

    Star Sign: Virgo.

    Worst Subject: Mathematics.

    According to the Haikyuu store in Japan, Bokuto has smaller hands than Akaashi because the latter has long fingers.

    His birthday is three days before the Autumnal equinox within the Japanese calendar.

    He is the third oldest third-year on the team after Tatsuki Washio and Yamato Sarukui.

    In the 10th Anniversary Chronicle book, it is revealed that Bokuto attended college and graduated, though Furudate has no idea why.

    According to Akaashi, Bokuto has at least 37 weaknesses.
        6.) He loves to show off.[22]
        37.) If he becomes too fixated on one thing, he'll completely forget how to do anything else.[23]

    In his second-year, at the Spring Tournament, he bought an unpopular t-shirt that reads: "The Wisdom of the Ace: First, the figure of the ace is one that inspires his allies. Second, he should shatter any wall. Third, he should hit any ball to his utmost ability." Unsurprisingly, he and Hinata find it cool.

    Akaashi states in the manga and English sub that Bokuto just missed being one of the top three aces while in the English dub, he says that Bokuto was once one of the top three but fell out for unknown reasons.
        In the manga, Suzumeda was overheard by Tsukishima to claim the reason Bokuto is not in the top three is because of his simplemindedness and mood swings, although this could possibly be her own assumptions.

    In a flashback Tsukishima has of the training camp, Kuroo and Bokuto were shown to have switched hairstyles.[24]

    Whenever he's in his 'emo mode', his hair deflates.

    He starred as himself in one of Ennoshita's roadshows called "The Day Armageddon Arrives on Earth".

    Despite being the captain, Bokuto wears #4 rather than the expected #1. This is most likely because Fukurōdani is the type of school where #4 is traditionally worn by the team's ace. His jersey in the manga and in the anime does have the mark that also signifies him as the team captain.

    He and Kotarō Hitaki share the same first name but not the same kanji and their pronunciation.

    Bokuto did not rank in the first popularity poll. In Haikyuu's second popularity poll, he ranked 18th with 3,084 votes.[25]

    Nomenclature:
        Kōtarō (光太郎) – 光 is a character meaning light while 太郎 (tarō) is a very common masculine name. The character 太 in common usage means thick or big. The last character 郎 simply means son.
        Bokuto (木兎) – Horned Owl
            The kanji literally mean tree and rabbit, but the combination is one of the ways to write horned owl, a species of owl.
