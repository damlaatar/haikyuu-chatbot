Tanaka has a shaved head with a widow's peak and sharp blue-grey eyes (golden brown in the manga colored illustrations) which led many to mistake him as a delinquent. Back in his first year at Karasuno, Tanaka had short fuzzy hair dyed in a light color (blond in the anime). He is average in height and has a lean, but strong build. He tends to make intimidating faces to try to scare others, which Daichi scolds him for as he claims they make him look foolish instead of scary.

Post-timeskip, Tanaka appears to have a bit more of a muscular build that most likely resulted from his career and also has let his hair grow out a bit in its natural dark color.

In A Party Reignited, he has dyed his hair a light color once more.
