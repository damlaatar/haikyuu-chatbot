Ryūnosuke Tanaka (Japanese: 田中 龍之介 Tanaka Ryūnosuke) for the majority of the series is a second-year student at Karasuno High. He is part of the Boys' Volleyball Club and plays as a starting wing spiker. Tanaka was known as the team's up-and-coming ace.

After graduating high school, he became a personal trainer and married Kiyoko Shimizu.
